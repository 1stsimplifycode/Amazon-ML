## Findings that change the plan

1. **Scale.** The train pool (S2+S3) has 10,320,219 records against 2,206,821 S1. The test pool has 9,969,589 against 1,732,544 S1. Any all-pairs step is impossible, so blocking must be sub-quadratic and fit in 16 GB RAM.
2. **GT exclusivity.** 7,638,365 listed IDs, all unique, so no S2/S3 record belongs to two S1. That is a hard structural constraint to enforce at decision time (one S2/S3 → at most one S1).
3. **Distractors.** 2,681,854 train S2+S3 records (26.0%) are not listed for any S1 (the check that all listed IDs exist is in 01).
4. **Singletons are rare:** 123,247 / 2,206,821 = 5.58%. Macro-F0.5 is dominated by multi-match S1 (mode 3 matches, max 11), so recall inside a cluster matters more than the brief's singleton emphasis implies.
5. **Blank addresses appear only in S2/S3** (about 3% of rows in every country, France included). S1 has no blanks. Names are never blank, but a few are literal `nan/null/NA` strings (6–61 per file); a default `pd.read_csv` would turn them into NaN.
6. **Exact duplicate (name, address, country) records with different IDs** exist inside S2 and S3 (16k–26k per file). 03 checks whether both copies sit in the same GT cluster.
7. **Native-script text** appears in India S2/S3 names and addresses (Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Bengali, Gujarati), including state names. Latin-only char n-grams cannot retrieve these, so transliteration is required. It has to be written in-house, because `unidecode` is GPL.
8. **Injected junk tokens** in S2/S3: `<<`, `>>`, `--`, `##`, `[Partners]`, `((Ltd))`, the literal `null` inside addresses, domain-style names (`unitedimpex.com`), word-order swaps, and typos (`Inevstmmnnt`, `Phyismans`).
9. **France:** test S1 has 259,452 French rows (15.0%). Training has none. The country mix also shifts: US is 60% of train S1 but 38% of test S1, and India goes from 40% to 47%.
10. **Disk:** C: has about 880 MB free. That blocks caching features, installing LightGBM and possibly writing `candidate_pairs.tsv` (hundreds of MB).
